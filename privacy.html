Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

CECS App ("the app", "we", "us") is a companion controller for the Central Exhibit Control System (CECS). It connects only to your own CECS desktop software on your local network or your private VPN to view and control exhibit hardware. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

2. Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan a pairing QR code displayed by your CECS desktop. Camera images are processed on your device and are never stored or transmitted.

3. Sign-in Credentials

The username and password you enter are sent only to your own CECS system in order to sign in. They are not sent to the developer or any third party.

4. Network Communication

The app communicates only with the CECS desktop address that you configure (on your local network or VPN). No data is sent to the developer's servers.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any information with third parties.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the app secure. Communication with your CECS system stays within your own network or private VPN.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is a professional control tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
